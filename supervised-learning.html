First, what is supervised learning?

We show the model many examples with the right answer (like a teacher with an answer key). It learns the pattern, then predicts answers for new examples it has never seen. There are two kinds of question it can answer.

CLASSIFICATION Pick a category

The answer is a label from a fixed list. Like a post-office sorter putting each letter in the right box.

“Will this customer leave? Yes / No”
  • Spam or not spam
  • Loan default: yes / no
  • Disease: positive / negative
  • Review sentiment: positive / neutral / negative

REGRESSION Predict a number

The answer is a quantity on a scale. Like a property valuer quoting a price after looking at a house.

“How many units will we sell next week? 1,240”
  • House price in ₹
  • Product demand (units)
  • Delivery time (minutes)
  • Student exam marks
